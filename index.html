Fresh air & cooling

Hot kitchen? The AC is often not the problem.

The kitchen exhaust pulls out a lot of air. The fresh air unit (FAHU) should replace most of it with treated, dry air. When it doesn't, hot and humid outside air is pulled in through every door and gap, and the AC can't keep up.

AC duct cleaning · Sanco FM

Clean air, not just cold air.

Dust, grease and moisture build up inside supply ducts. In our climate that layer grows mould, and every time the AC runs it is blown into the room — a common trigger for allergies and asthma at home.

Case study · Retrofit & fresh air

Allo Beirut, Al Hessa — fixing the heat at its source.

A 300 m² restaurant, too hot all summer. The exhaust took out 14,000 CFM while an untreated fresh air system put back 7,500, so about 6,500 CFM of hot, humid air was pulled in. We designed and installed a 12,000 CFM treated FAHU.

≈ 86tons of fresh-air load moved off the split units onto the treated FAHU
≈ 140kkWh of electricity saved per year (estimate)
AED 60kestimated saving per year
~5 yrspayback on the retrofit

Estimate based on 1.6 kW/TR before, splits cycling plus a 40 TR FAHU at 1.1 kW/TR after, AED 0.44/kWh. Indicative only.
