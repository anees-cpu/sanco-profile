How kitchen exhaust fires start

Cleaning more often won’t stop a fire.

However often the hood and duct are cleaned, a water-mist system or ESP that isn’t maintained can still cause a fire. The water-mist hood is there to stop sparks and burning grease at the hood. If it isn’t working, nothing stops them reaching the duct.

01Water-mist hood not workingThe water mist keeps carbon and grease from building up inside the hood and stops sparks from charcoal grills reaching the exhaust duct. When it fails, both go straight in, even in a freshly cleaned system.
02ESP not workingThe electrostatic precipitator stops trapping grease and smoke, so it goes straight into the duct.
03Exhaust fan switched off or brokenHeat can't escape from the hood and duct. A snapped belt or seized bearing is enough.
04Duct not cleaned on timeFOG builds up layer by layer inside the duct.
05Filters missing or cloggedGrease bypasses the filters, or airflow drops and heat stays in the kitchen.
